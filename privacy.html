Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains how Whitespace Consulting ("we", "us" or "our") handles personal information when you visit whitespace.consulting or contact us through this website.

We use information you provide to respond to your enquiry and discuss our services. You can contact us about your information using our contact form.

Information we collect

When you use our contact form, we collect your name, email address and the contents of your message, including any business or project details you choose to share. We may also keep follow-up correspondence with you.

Providing information is voluntary. The form requires a name, email address and message so we can understand and reply to your enquiry. If you do not provide these details, you will not be able to submit the form.

Our hosting and service providers may process technical information when you visit or submit a form, such as your IP address, browser and device information, pages requested, referring page, and the date and time of requests. This helps deliver the website, maintain security and prevent abuse.

Please do not include identity documents, financial details, health information or other sensitive personal information in the contact form.

How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and communicate with you about your request.
  • To understand your requirements and discuss or arrange consulting services.
  • To operate, secure and troubleshoot the website and prevent spam or misuse.
  • To maintain relevant business records and meet legal obligations.

If a consulting engagement involves additional personal information, we will explain the relevant handling arrangements as part of that engagement.

Service providers and sharing

We use service providers to operate this website and receive enquiries:

  • Railway hosts the website and may process request and diagnostic data to deliver and maintain it. See Railway's privacy policy.
  • Formspree processes and delivers contact form submissions and provides spam protection. Your submitted details and associated technical information are processed by Formspree. See Formspree's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts, Unsplash and unpkg supply fonts, images and the icon library used on our home page. Your browser connects directly to these services, which receive technical information such as your IP address and request details. See the privacy policies for Google, Unsplash and Cloudflare, which provides unpkg's delivery network.

Information may also be handled by providers used for business email and communication when we reply to you. We limit sharing to what is needed for the relevant purpose. We may disclose information where required or permitted by law. We do not sell personal information.

Overseas processing

Our service providers may process or store information outside New Zealand. When we disclose personal information overseas, we take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, as required by the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

Cookies and external content

Our website code does not set first-party cookies or include advertising or analytics tracking. Third-party services, including form submission and spam-protection services, may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies. You can manage cookies through your browser settings; blocking them may affect those services.

Links to other websites are subject to those websites' own privacy policies.

Storage, security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access or disclosure. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

We keep personal information only for as long as needed for the purposes described here, including relevant business and legal record-keeping requirements. When it is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete it or make it no longer identifiable.

Your rights and privacy concerns

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020, you can ask for access to the personal information we hold about you and request a correction. If we do not make a correction you request, you can ask us to attach a statement of the correction sought.

For access or correction requests, or to raise a privacy concern, use our contact form and address your message to the privacy contact at Whitespace Consulting. We may need to verify your identity before providing personal information.

If you are not satisfied with our response to a privacy concern, you can contact the New Zealand Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our website or information-handling practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
