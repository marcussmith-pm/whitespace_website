03 Workforce Management

BPO operations · Systems integration

Connect the roster with what happens on the floor.

A workforce operations layer that brings schedules, attendance and leave into connected supervisor and employee workflows, alongside the organisation’s existing tools.

Project focus
Roster ingestion, attendance visibility and leave workflows
Designed for
Workforce teams, supervisors and employees

The problem

The information a supervisor needs lives in different places.

A roster can say someone is scheduled while another system records approved leave or an incomplete attendance session. Supervisors need to reconcile those differences; employees need access to their schedule and leave requests. The product connects those views while keeping the source systems authoritative.

The working system

How the workflow fits together.

  1. 01

    Bring together

    Import Excel rosters and connect employee, leave and Genesys attendance information.

  2. 02

    Reconcile

    Match records to employees and flag schedule conflicts or incomplete sessions.

  3. 03

    Act

    Support leave review and notify employees when their shifts change.

  4. 04

    Review

    Compare scheduled and actual activity through operational reporting.

Product decisions

Define the boundaries around the work.

Work with the existing operation

The system is an integration layer around Genesys, PaySpace and Deel. It carries the source information into useful workflows without claiming to replace those products.

Give each audience a useful view

Supervisors use a web dashboard for review and reporting. Employees use the mobile application for schedules, leave balances and requests.

Engineering the system

Make the detail hold together.

Handle changes and exceptions

Schedule comparison identifies added, cancelled and changed shifts. Conflict detection distinguishes approved leave from pending requests so the operational response can differ.

Reconcile identities and time

Attendance processing matches Genesys identities to employee records, handles multiple sessions and tracks incomplete records. Schedule handling includes explicit time-zone conversion.

Proposed extension Agentic AI in the solution

An agent that investigates workforce exceptions.

A proposed workforce operations agent could monitor discrepancies across rosters, attendance and leave, then gather the evidence a supervisor needs to respond. Its role would be to investigate an exception and prepare a useful follow-up within the existing workforce tools.

Assemble the operational picture

The agent would retrieve the scheduled shift, matched attendance sessions, leave status and recent roster changes for the exception under review.

Prepare a supervisor briefing

It could explain the discrepancy with links to the source records, highlight missing information and draft a clarification request or follow-up task.

  1. 01

    Observe

    A schedule, leave or attendance discrepancy appears.

  2. 02

    Investigate

    Bring the relevant records and recent changes together.

  3. 03

    Propose

    Prepare an evidence-linked briefing and a draft follow-up.

  4. 04

    Review

    A supervisor checks the context and decides the response.

Proposed extension, not an implemented feature of the reviewed application. Supervisors would retain staffing and leave decisions, and upstream systems would remain authoritative.

In practice

What the system enables.

Supervisors can review schedules alongside leave and attendance evidence. Employees can access their workforce information and follow leave requests through the mobile experience.

Upstream workforce and HR systems remain the sources of truth. This project description does not claim measured savings or workforce performance improvements.
