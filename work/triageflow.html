01 TriageFlow

Healthcare · AI decision support

Free up clinical capacity with AI-assisted referral triage.

TriageFlow uses AI to assess referrals against configured pathways and clinical guidance, then presents structured recommendations for clinician review. The aim is to free up senior clinical time while keeping clinical judgement and accountability within the workflow.

Project focus
Referral intake, clinical review and configurable routing
Designed for
Clinical reviewers and service administrators

The problem

Demand exceeds capacity. Triage takes clinical time.

When demand exceeds clinical capacity, referral triage determines which patients need attention first. Senior medical officers must review the information, assess urgency and identify the appropriate care pathway. That work places a substantial demand on the clinicians whose time the health system needs for patient care.

Product decisions

Define the boundaries around the work.

Make recommendations reviewable

The application separates AI recommendations from recorded clinician feedback. Reviewers can work with priorities, pathways and dispositions rather than a single unexplained score.

Respect the service context

Departments can configure their pathways and intake rules. Routing logic considers department suitability and regional service availability instead of assuming one process fits every service.

Engineering the system

Make the detail hold together.

Guidance and feedback

The triage service retrieves published clinical guidance and can incorporate previous reviewer feedback when constructing an assessment.

A traceable decision

Structured feedback validation and audit events connect the recorded decision with the referral and reviewer. The system includes explicit states for review drafts that need clarification.

AI inside the solution

AI performs defined tasks within the referral workflow.

The AI services work on the referral itself: selecting a suitable configured pathway and producing a structured triage recommendation. The application supplies the available pathways and relevant guidance, validates the response and brings the assessment into the clinical review process.

Find a pathway within the service rules

When the intake workflow has no existing pathway hint, an AI service evaluates the referral against configured candidates and their operational rules. It can return a matching pathway or leave the selection unresolved; the application rejects a pathway outside the supplied options.

Prepare an assessment for clinical review

The triage service combines referral information with retrieved clinical guidance and relevant reviewer feedback to propose a priority, pathway and reasoning. The clinician can approve or modify the assessment and record the rationale.

The AI carries out bounded assessment tasks. Configured rules control routing, and the clinical review workflow records the human decision; this is not an autonomous clinical decision-maker.

In practice

What the system enables.

The aim is to reduce the time clinicians spend assembling and assessing referral information. AI prepares an assessment and supporting reasoning for review, while clinicians retain the ability to approve or modify the recommendation and record their judgement. Administrators configure the pathways and service rules around that work.

Time and cost savings are potential benefits; quantified claims require evaluation evidence. This project description does not establish clinical accuracy, regulatory certification or patient outcomes.
